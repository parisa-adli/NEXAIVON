# NEXAIVON

An online shop for technology products, with a public storefront for shoppers and a back office for the store's staff.

## Language

### Areas of the site

**Storefront**:
The public shopping pages a shopper browses and buys from: home, product listing, product detail, cart, sign-in and complete-profile, together with the header around them.
_Avoid_: User side, front, website

**Account area**:
The signed-in shopper's own pages for their details and past payments.
_Avoid_: Profile panel, dashboard

**Admin panel**:
The back office where staff manage products, categories, coupons, payments and users.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, back end

### Kinds of change

**Restyle**:
A change to how existing information and existing actions look and are arranged on the page, leaving what is fetched and what happens on an action untouched.
_Avoid_: Redesign, UI upgrade, revamp
